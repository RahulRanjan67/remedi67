Platform Features

Donate Medicines

Surplus and unneeded medicines can be donated with batch number and expiry checks.

NGO Quality Verification

Accredited non-profits verify sealed status, batch, and safe shelf life before listing.

City Inventory Search

Browse live supply in your city with category filters and urgency tags.

Structured Request Flow

Request exact amounts needed with direct, NGO, or pharmacy pickup options.

Accredited Pharmacy Network

Exchange medicines through registered community pharmacies acting as drop points.

Urgent Needs Board

Post critical shortages and automatically match with newly verified donations.
